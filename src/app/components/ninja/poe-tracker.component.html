About DelveScout

DelveScout is a professional analytical tool for Path of Exile players, designed to optimize Delve farming strategy.

It analyzes real-time market prices from poe.ninja , calculates biome profitability based on fossil drop rates, and computes the Expected Value (EV) of valuable boss drops from Delve encounter bosses (Aul, Kurgal, Ahuatotli).

Features historical cross-league price comparisons (e.g., Mirage vs Allflame ) to track and analyze fossil value trends across different league timelines.

Fossil and biome data are based on up-to-date tables from the official PoE Wiki.
